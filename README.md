# Festival Rental Operations

A Fake Business Inc. project.

**Real software. Fake companies.**

This repository follows the evolution of a fictional live-event equipment rental company specializing in EDM, nightlife, festivals, concerts, and experiential events.

The company begins with a fragmented manual operating process built around email, spreadsheets, shared documents, and accounting software. Custom software will be introduced incrementally as real operational problems emerge.

## Current State

The company currently manages rental inventory and reservations primarily through legacy spreadsheets.

The first engineering problem is determining whether a requested quantity of equipment is actually available for a specified rental period.

## Development Philosophy

- Understand the business problem before choosing technology.
- Build in small, working increments.
- Introduce complexity only when a business requirement justifies it.
- Prefer existing services for commodity infrastructure.
- Build custom software where company-specific business logic provides value.
- Preserve the legacy process so the evolution of the system remains visible.

## Company #1

A small regional live-event equipment rental company serving event promoters, venues, production companies, DJs, concerts, festivals, and nightlife events.

Its inventory includes equipment such as silent-disco headsets, audio equipment, radios, lighting, screens, staging components, charging stations, lounge equipment, and unusual experiential festival installations.
